Sutlej River

The Sutlej River[a] or the Satluj River is a major river in Asia, flowing through China, India and Pakistan, and is the longest of the five major rivers of the Punjab region. It is also known as Satadru;[3] and is the easternmost tributary of the Indus River. The combination of the Sutlej and Chenab rivers in the plains of Punjab forms the Panjnad, which finally flows into the Indus River at Mithankot. In India, the Bhakra Dam is built on the Sutlej River to provide irrigation and other facilities to the states of Punjab, Rajasthan and Haryana.
