Ravi River

The Ravi River is a river in South Asia that flows through northwestern India and eastern Pakistan forming the boundary between the two nations for about 50 miles before travelling westwards across eastern Pakistan. It is one of five major rivers of the Punjab region.

Length

720 km (447 miles)

Origin

Rises in the Himalayas (Bara Bhangal, Kangra/Chamba district) in Himachal Pradesh, India.

The Ravi River starts in the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh, India, and ends in Pakistan by emptying into the Chenab River near Ahmadpur Sial (read more)
