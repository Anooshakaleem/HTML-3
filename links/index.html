Rivers in Pakistan

There are several major rivers in Pakistan, including the Indus River, which is the longest river in the country and flows through the entire length of the nation.

The River is a vital water source for agriculture and supports the livelihoods of millions of people in Pakistan. It originates in Tibet and flows through India before entering Pakistan, where it eventually empties into the Arabian Sea.

A river is a natural, flowing stream of freshwater that moves from higher ground like mountains or hills down to a lower elevation, such as an ocean, sea, or lake. Rivers are powered by gravity and fed by rain, melting snow, and underground springs.
