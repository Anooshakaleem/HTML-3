Jhelum River

The Jhelum River is a river that flows through the Indian-administered territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Pakistani province of Punjab. It is one of the five rivers that give Punjab its name, which means "Land of Five Rivers." The Jhelum River originates from the Indian-administered territory of Jammu and Kashmir and flows through the Wular Lake before entering Pakistan.

The river has historical significance, as it was mentioned in ancient texts and has been a site of various historical events. The Jhelum River is also known for its scenic beauty, with lush green valleys and picturesque landscapes along its course. It plays a crucial role in irrigation and hydroelectric power generation in the region.

The Jhelum River originates from the Verinag Spring, located at the foothills of the Pir Panjal Range, near Anantnag in the southeastern part of the Kashmir Valley. Three significant tributaries-the Sandran, Bringi, and Arapath rivers-join from its right bank.
